## At a glance

| Capability | What the product documents | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan and grouping | Screenshot sizes, review stacks and duplicate/visual-match groups. | Estimates are not a promise of immediately available device storage. |
| Selection | Inspect items and use group selection or Keep Newest where offered. | A visual match can still contain a meaningful difference; inspect before selecting deletion. |
| Deletion | Selected items are removed through the Photos confirmation flow. | Photos Recently Deleted behavior is separate from the app; confirm recovery availability before depending on it. |
